The Tourism Tightrope

Malaysia's tourism industry generates RM291 billion and employs 3.54 million people. But beneath the headline numbers, not every state shares equally in this boom — and some are far more exposed to collapse than others.

By Soo Jun Hui  |  May 2026  |  Data: DOSM · Tourism Malaysia · data.gov.my

Act 1

The Engine

Tourism contributes 15.1% to Malaysia's GDP — a recovery from the near-total collapse during the pandemic. But how stable is this engine, and who benefits from it?

Who Benefits?

Act 3

What Kind of Tourism?

Volume is only half the story. The character of tourism — where it comes from and how concentrated it is — determines how resilient each state really is. A state that receives millions of visitors from a single source country is far more exposed to external shocks than one with a diverse visitor mix.

Key insight: Singapore alone accounts for nearly 50% of all international arrivals into Malaysia. This makes Malaysia's inbound tourism highly sensitive to a single neighbour's economic conditions and travel policies.
